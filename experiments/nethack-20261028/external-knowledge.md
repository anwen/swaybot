# External knowledge (from NetHack Wiki, retrieved via web search)

- To eat food lying on the ground, stand on the SAME square as the food and
  use the 'eat' action. You can eat directly from the floor - you do NOT need
  to pick it up first.
- If you get "You don't have anything to eat", you are NOT standing on the
  food square. Move onto the food (%) first, then eat.
- If you get "There is nothing here to pick up", you are on the wrong square -
  adjust position before trying again.
- Eating is the goal in MiniHack-Eat: once you eat the food you win. Do not
  waste turns on other actions.
