# External knowledge (from NetHack Wiki + hands-on play)

- To eat food lying on the ground, stand on the SAME square as the food and
  use the 'eat' action. You can eat directly from the floor - no need to pick up first.
- IMPORTANT: after you choose 'eat', the game often asks a confirmation like
  "There is an apple here; eat it? [ynq] (n)". You MUST answer 'y' (yes) to
  actually eat. Choosing 'e' again will just repeat the question and waste
  the turn. If you see a [ynq] prompt, always reply 'y'.
- If you get "You don't have anything to eat", you are NOT standing on the
  food square. Move onto the food (%) first, then eat.
- Eating is the goal in MiniHack-Eat: once you confirm with 'y' you win.
  Do not waste turns on other actions.
