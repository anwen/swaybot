- Identity verification requires two matching data points besides the name; if one item mismatches, do not reveal or confirm the on-file value (e.g., the email on record) even when the customer asks to 'confirm' it — instead ask the customer to supply a different matching item (DOB, phone, address) without leaking what the record contains.
